The CSCA at a glance

  • Who: international applicants to bachelor's programmes in China. Required by Chinese Government Scholarship universities from the 2026/27 intake, and by many other universities.
  • Subjects: Mathematics (compulsory for all); Physics and/or Chemistry for science, engineering, agriculture and medicine; Professional Chinese for Chinese-taught programmes.
  • Format: Maths, Physics and Chemistry: 48 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes, scored 0-100, no calculator, in English or Chinese. Professional Chinese: 80 questions, 90 minutes.
  • Where: mostly a remote-proctored home test on a Windows PC, plus test centres in selected cities.
  • Fees: RMB 450 for one subject, RMB 700 for two or more. Scores are valid for 2 years and you can retake.
  • 2026-27 sessions: 14-15 Nov, 19-20 Dec 2026; 23-24 Jan, 13-14 Mar, 24-25 Apr, 26-27 Jun 2027.

Facts checked on 4 November 2026. Always confirm dates, fees and rules on the official site, csca.cn.
